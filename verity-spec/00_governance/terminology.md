## 1. Core Platform Lexicon

The following terms represent canonical business concepts in Verity. Developers, API contracts, and user interface elements must use these exact terms, avoiding slang or developer jargon (such as "jobs" or "tasks" when referring to work orders).

---

### Work Hierarchy Terms

#### GOV-TER-001: Work (Work Order)
*   **Definition**: The canonical execution unit representing a single committed service obligation to be performed at a location. It has an independent state machine (Draft to Completed) and is the root of execution.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-002: Request
*   **Definition**: An uncommitted intake ticket generated by a customer or automated alert. A Request is evaluated by dispatchers and either rejected, resolved directly, or converted into a committed Work Order.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-003: ChecklistItem
*   **Definition**: A discrete sub-step checklist item within a single Work Order. A ChecklistItem has no independent state machine (is open/completed/skipped) and cannot be scheduled independently. It exists strictly as an aggregate child of the Work Order.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-004: Activity
*   **Definition**: A structured change record or communication log entry tracking history (e.g. email sent, call logged, status transitioned by Supervisor X).
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-005: Appointment
*   **Definition**: A scheduled time-locked block on a Resource's calendar, representing the time commitment for a Work Order or event.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-006: Project
*   **Definition**: A structural container grouping multiple parent-child related Work Orders together for long-term tracking and milestone progress.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

---

### Resource & Identity Terms

#### GOV-TER-007: Resource
*   **Definition**: A capacity-constrained unit available for shift/work allocation. A Resource can represent a human worker (linked to a User/Party) or physical equipment/room (linked to an Asset).
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-008: Employee
*   **Definition**: An HR-specific contract profile storing wages, leaves, tax records, and internal company documents. An Employee is linked to a human Party profile, but exists outside the operational Scheduling layer.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-009: Asset
*   **Definition**: A physical piece of equipment or machinery (e.g., HVAC unit, vehicle, fire extinguisher) registered as a structural trackable at a Location. An Asset may be linked to a Resource profile to allow scheduling its capacity.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

---

### Organizational Terms

#### GOV-TER-010: Organization
*   **Definition**: A logical business unit or legal entity under a Tenant. Organizations can be nested hierarchically (Parent Org, child Branch).
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-011: Tenant
*   **Definition**: The primary database-level isolation boundary representing a client account (e.g., ACME Facilities Corp). A Tenant contains one or more Organizations, Users, and Resources.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

---

### Customer Pipeline Terms

#### GOV-TER-012: Lead
*   **Definition**: An unqualified prospect representing an unverified sales inquiry.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-013: Customer (Account)
*   **Definition**: A qualified company or commercial client entity that purchases services from the Tenant.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-014: Contact
*   **Definition**: A specific human individual associated with a Customer Account, representing a primary point of communication.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

---

### Event & Audit Terms

#### GOV-TER-015: Event
*   **Definition**: An immutable, chronological record of a significant state change emitted by a capability and broadcast to the platform's Event Bus (e.g. `work_order.job.completed`).
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-016: Log
*   **Definition**: Unstructured developer-facing output lines generated by processes for system debugging and troubleshooting. System Logs must never be mixed with Business Events.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`

#### GOV-TER-017: Place (Address Model)
*   **Definition**: A structured representation of physical coordinates and mail routing attributes. We distinguish:
    *   `Location`: An operational site containing geofence boundaries where work occurs.
    *   `Address`: A communication profile (billing or shipping) mapped to a Party, referring to a specific Place record.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN]`
