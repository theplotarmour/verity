## 1. Hierarchy of Authority

To resolve logical, architectural, and requirement conflicts during development, all engineers and automated systems must adhere to a strict, non-negotiable hierarchy of authority. Higher levels override lower levels without exception.

```text
  Level 1: Explicit Verity Constitutional Decisions (User Directives)
     │
     ▼
  Level 2: Verity Master Bible (verity-bible/)
     │
     ▼
  Level 3: Explicit Approved Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)
     │
     ▼
  Level 4: Verity Master Platform Specification (This Corpus)
     │
     ▼
  Level 5: Verified External & Domain Research
     │
     ▼
  Level 6: Odoo-Derived Reference PRD
     │
     ▼
  Level 7: Legacy Verity Codebase (Non-Authoritative)
     │
     ▼
  Level 8: Assumptions & Model Inference
```

## 2. Authority Level Definitions

### GOV-AUT-001: Level 1 — Explicit Verity Constitutional Decisions
*   **Description**: Direct, explicit mandates delivered by the product owner or user (e.g. "Do not begin transforming the Odoo PRD into the Verity PRD yet").
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE]`

### GOV-AUT-002: Level 2 — Verity Master Bible
*   **Description**: The constitutional volumes under `verity-bible/` defining Verity's philosophy, non-negotiables, core primitives, and security boundaries.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE]`

### GOV-AUT-003: Level 3 — Approved ADRs
*   **Description**: Formal, finalized architectural decision documents stored in `verity-spec/17_decisions/adr/`.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE]`

### GOV-AUT-004: Level 4 — Verity Master Platform Specification
*   **Description**: The detailed specification folders and files under `verity-spec/` outlining platform schemas, capabilities, UI experience, and sync engines.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: RESEARCH_REQUIRED]`

### GOV-AUT-005: Level 5 — Verified External & Domain Research
*   **Description**: Technical standards (e.g. W3C, RFCs, spatial math specifications) and verified sector domain rules (e.g. safety inspection regulations) cited explicitly in the spec.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: RESEARCH_REQUIRED]`

### GOV-AUT-006: Level 6 — Odoo-Derived Reference PRD
*   **Description**: The inventory of Odoo's business rules and schemas documented in `odoo-prd/`. Used strictly as a breadth checklist, never as an architectural constraint.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: RESEARCH_REQUIRED]`

### GOV-AUT-007: Level 7 — Legacy Codebase
*   **Description**: The existing codebase state. It is considered a rough draft and holds zero authority. Legacy code must be refactored to align with Level 2 and Level 4 specs.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: RESEARCH_REQUIRED]`

### GOV-AUT-008: Level 8 — Model Inference
*   **Description**: Extrapolations or standard software patterns generated by AI models without explicit source evidence. These are permitted only in the absence of higher-level specifications and must be flagged as `[INFERRED]`.
*   **Status**: `[UNKNOWN_REASON: SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE]`
