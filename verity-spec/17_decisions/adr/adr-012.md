# Architectural Decision Record

## ADR-012: Verity Visual System v2 — Brand-Sheet Reconciliation

*   **Decision ID**: ADR-012
*   **Question**: Which values are Verity's canonical neutrals, default accent and semantic colours, given that the product owner has supplied a document titled *VERITY BRAND IDENTITY* whose palette contradicts the accent default currently written into `CLAUDE.md` and `src/server/platform/accent.ts`?
*   **Context**: `design/verity asthetics.png` is a brand identity sheet. Its UI style guide labels five swatch roles — Primary, Text, Surface, Border, Background — and names the palette `#0F1115`, `#1C1F24`, `#2A2E33`, `#E6E8EB`, `#F7F8FA`, `#00D1B2`, with `#00D1B2` occupying the **Primary** role. It specifies Inter (Thin / Light / Regular / Medium), an outline icon style, the hourglass mark in three monochrome variations, and the hourglass as app icon and favicon.

    `design/light theme.webp` and `design/dark theme.webp` show the same product screen in both themes, rendered in that Primary colour, with glass on the elevated product panel and solid material on the dense table.

    `CLAUDE.md` states the opposite: that the default accent is Warm Sand Gold `#D4A017` "because that is the brand's colour", that the mint boards are "a preset, not a rebrand", and it lists **"do not replace gold with teal"** as an explicit anti-regression rule. `accent.ts` ships `DEFAULT_ACCENT = "#D4A017"` and a `Verity Mint` preset at `#0FA894` — a value that appears in neither the brand sheet nor the boards.

    This is a genuine authority conflict, raised as **QB-1** in `implementation/work-plan-2026-08-26.md` §15 and deliberately left unresolved there rather than settled silently.
*   **Affected Concepts**: Experience System tokens; default accent; accent preset list; neutral foundation; semantic colour independence; brand mark colour; app icon and favicon; WCAG AA verification
*   **Evidence**:

    First, the brand sheet is a **primary Verity design decision**, supplied by the product owner, and the work plan's reference hierarchy (§2) places `design/` above implementation and above the Steep extraction in the same folder. The sheet is titled *VERITY BRAND IDENTITY* and names `#00D1B2` Primary in its own words. No comparable document names `#D4A017`.

    Second, `CLAUDE.md` is repository instruction, not constitutional authority. It sits below an explicit Verity decision in the stated authority order. Its gold clause records an earlier reading of the design source `Verity App.dc.html`, which is an implementation artefact, not the brand sheet.

    Third, the change is **narrow by construction**. The accent architecture built under ADR-011 — one seed, a `color-mix` 50→900 ladder, contrast-computed fill/ink pairs, presets plus custom hex — is unchanged. Only the seed's default value moves. That the system absorbs a hue change by editing one constant is the evidence that the architecture was right.

    Fourth, `#00D1B2` is a **light** accent and fails badly as text: 1.9:1 on white. The computed ladder already exists precisely for this, and measures the corrected pair rather than assuming it — light fill `#02B99E` with dark ink at 7.00:1, dark fill `#6BE4D2` with dark ink at 11.32:1.

    Fifth, a teal accent sits near the semantic success green, which ADR-011 requires to stay independent of the accent. Measured, the shipped success `#3F7D53` and the new accent are close enough in hue to read as one signal in a status column — the exact failure D4 exists to prevent. This is **QB-2**, and it is answered here rather than deferred.

    Sixth, reconciling neutrals to the brand sheet exposed a **pre-existing** accessibility failure unrelated to this decision: semantic warning `#9C7016` measures 4.03:1 on the current canvas and 4.17:1 on the brand-sheet canvas. It has never cleared AA.
*   **Evidence Location**: `design/verity asthetics.png`; `design/light theme.webp`; `design/dark theme.webp`; `design/reference/verity-inventory.html` (board reimplementation); `implementation/work-plan-2026-08-26.md` §3 F1–F4, §15 QB-1/QB-2/QD-1; `src/app/globals.css`; `src/server/platform/accent.ts`; `verity-spec/17_decisions/adr/adr-011.md`
*   **Options**:
    *   *Option A*: Keep `#D4A017` as default; treat the brand sheet as one preset among ten. Preserves `CLAUDE.md` unchanged; requires treating a document titled *BRAND IDENTITY* as non-authoritative about the brand.
    *   *Option B*: Adopt the brand sheet as canonical — `#00D1B2` becomes the default accent, brand-sheet neutrals become the foundation, gold remains a preset — and update `CLAUDE.md` in the same change so instructions and code agree.
    *   *Option C*: Adopt the brand sheet **and** treat the accent as fixed brand identity, removing configurability.
*   **Recommendation**: Option B.
*   **Status**: `ACCEPTED`
*   **Owner**: Product Owner
*   **Resolution**: Adopt Option B. The product owner directed that the design language of `design/verity asthetics.png`, `light theme.webp` and `dark theme.webp` be applied to the Verity application. That directive answers QB-1 in favour of the brand sheet.

    **1 — Default accent.** `DEFAULT_ACCENT` becomes `#00D1B2`. The preset formerly named *Verity Mint* at `#0FA894` is corrected to the brand-sheet value; `Warm Sand Gold #D4A017` is retained as a preset, not deleted. Custom hex remains. The full preset system, the tonal ladder and the computed contrast pairs are untouched.

    **2 — Accent is still not brand.** ADR-011's separation stands (D9). The mark is monochrome and is never recoloured by the selected accent. The app icon and favicon carry the mark in brand ink, not in accent — correcting `icon.svg` and `apple-icon.svg`, which painted the mark gold and therefore made the identity a function of an earlier accent choice.

    **3 — Neutral foundation.** The brand sheet's values become canonical (QD-1): canvas `#F7F8FA` / `#0F1115`, primary text `#0F1115` / `#E6E8EB`, elevated dark surface `#1C1F24`, with `#2A2E33` as the dark control tone. Values that fail AA against these grounds are adjusted and recorded here, never shipped as-is.

    **4 — Semantic separation from the accent (QB-2).** Success moves away from teal to a leaf green — `#2F7A3E` light (4.97:1) / `#7CC98A` dark (9.52:1) — so accent-tinted UI and success status cannot be confused. Warning moves from `#9C7016` to `#8A6212` (5.15:1 light), correcting the pre-existing AA failure named in Evidence. Danger and info are unchanged and continue to clear AA on the new grounds.

    **5 — Both themes remain designed, not inverted.** D7 and D8 stand. Dark values are authored per surface. No inversion filter is introduced.

    **6 — Glass is retained.** This ADR does not revisit ADR-011's material decision. The glass ladder, the four blurred surface kinds, the reduced-transparency fallback and the composited-contrast constraint all continue in force.

    **Relationship to ADR-011.** ADR-011 is superseded **in part** — its clause "the default accent is Warm Sand Gold `#D4A017`" only. Every other clause, including the entire glass material system and all six constraints, is retained. ADR-011's `Superseded By` field records this partial supersession.

    **`CLAUDE.md` is updated in the same change.** Leaving "do not replace gold with teal" in force while shipping `#00D1B2` would put the repository's own instructions in contradiction with its code.

    **Deviations from the boards, deliberate and recorded.**

    | Board property | Shipped | Reason |
    |---|---|---|
    | Accent used directly as status green | Success retuned away from teal | ADR-011 semantic independence; two signals must not read as one |
    | Board's inventory domain | Not built | Verity has no inventory capability and must not grow one (QD-2 — composition is the template, content is not) |
    | Board table rows on solid material | Retained solid | ADR-011: dense data never takes glass |

    **Out of scope.** Shell composition, scroll ownership and the sidebar overflow matrix (work plan §5.6, §5.7) are Phase 1 implementation tasks governed by this ADR's tokens; they are not decided here.
