# Architectural Decision Record

## ADR-004: Decoupling of Address and Spatial Location

*   **Decision ID**: ADR-004
*   **Question**: Should physical addresses be structurally unified with operational Location site geofences?
*   **Context**: Mismatch between mailing addresses (CRM/Partners) and operational physical coordinate boundaries.
*   **Affected Concepts**: `Location`, `Address`
*   **Evidence**: Odoo res.partner address models; WMS stock.location.
*   **Evidence Location**: Odoo res.partner address models; WMS stock.location.
*   **Options**:
    *   *Option A*: Location includes street address, zip, and geofence radius.
    *   *Option B*: Decouple Address, Place, Location, and Geofence policies.
*   **Recommendation**: Option B.
*   **Status**: `ACCEPTED`
*   **Owner**: Product Owner
*   **Resolution**: Adopt Option B. Model them as four distinct concepts:
    - `Place`: Real-world spatial/physical identity.
    - `Address`: Representation for communications and mailing.
    - `Location`: Operational spatial context.
    - `Geofence`: Spatial policy/constraint.
    Geofences are policies, not Locations themselves. Locations represent physical regions but do not inherit mailing address details directly.
*   **Supersedes**: None
*   **Superseded By**: None
*   **Confidence**: `HIGH`
