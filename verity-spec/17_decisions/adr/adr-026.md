# Architectural Decision Record

## ADR-026: Layered Glass Content Surfaces and Visual Field

* **Decision ID**: ADR-026
* **Question**: Should Verity's Apple-craft material language remain limited to
  structural chrome, or should cards and panels across the product use a
  richer layered visual treatment?
* **Context**: ADR-024 intentionally kept dense content solid while the shell
  returned to glass. The resulting product is coherent but visually too flat
  across many operational pages: cards, panels and section boundaries do not
  consistently express depth, color temperature or visual focus. The product
  owner has now explicitly directed a richer iOS-like glass treatment, with
  colors, shades and visual anchors across pages while retaining Verity's
  identity and configurable accent system.
* **Decision**: Adopt a layered material field across Verity. `Surface` defaults
  to the glass-card material; the atmospheric field and grain remain active;
  shared cards inherit blur, translucent gradients, edge highlights and soft
  elevation. `verity-solid` remains available and is the required choice for
  dense tables, long-form text, forms and destructive confirmation when
  opacity materially improves contrast or decision safety. Glass is never
  applied per row, badge or status indicator.
* **Visual rules**:
  1. Color comes from the configurable accent seed and semantic tokens; no
     page hard-codes a brand or accent color.
  2. Depth comes from layered tint, blur, edge light and shadow—not arbitrary
     gradients on every child.
  3. Visual anchors may use atmospheric fields, icon chips, charts and empty
     states, but must support the page's operational purpose.
  4. Light/dark parity, WCAG AA, reduced motion and reduced transparency remain
     mandatory.
  5. The Verity mark, wordmark and typography remain unchanged.
* **Status**: `ACCEPTED`
* **Owner**: Product Owner
* **Supersedes**: ADR-024's content-surface limitation only; ADR-024's accent,
  motion, brand and accessibility constraints remain in force.
* **Implementation**: `src/components/ui/primitives.tsx` and
  `src/app/globals.css` establish the default. Individual page exceptions must
  use `solid={true}` with a content-density reason.
